About FocusLock

FocusLock is an Android app that creates a simple barrier between you and distracting apps.

The problem

Apps are built to keep you scrolling. Willpower alone often loses.

Mission

Make distraction harder and focus easier, without taking control away from you.

Philosophy

You set the rules. FocusLock helps you keep them when it matters.

What makes it different

  • Unlocking takes a deliberate challenge you choose.
  • Your usage data stays on your device.
  • Calls and emergencies are never blocked.
