About

About FocusLock

FocusLock is an Android app that puts a simple barrier between you and distracting apps.

The problem

Apps are built to keep you scrolling

Feeds, short videos and games are designed to be hard to put down. Willpower alone often loses.

Who it is for

Anyone who wants more control

Students, workers, creators and anyone who opens an app without meaning to.

How it works

Limits, a block screen, a pause

You pick apps and set daily limits. FocusLock tracks usage, warns you, and covers the app with a block screen when time is up. To get more time you complete a challenge you chose.

  1. Select. Choose the apps you want to control.
  2. Set. Set a daily time limit for each one.
  3. Monitor. FocusLock tracks how long each app is used.
  4. Warn. You get a heads-up when a limit is close.
  5. Block. When time is up, a block screen covers the app.
  6. Decide. Stay locked in, or complete a challenge you chose to unlock.

Mission

Make distraction harder, focus easier

Without taking control away from you.

Philosophy

You set the rules

FocusLock helps you keep them when it matters.

Privacy first

No account, data on your phone

There is no sign-up. Your limits and usage stay on your device. We do not need an account because nothing has to be synced. Read the privacy page.

Why Android

Where the tools are

FocusLock uses Android features such as usage access and the Accessibility service to see which app is open. That is why it is built for Android.

Difference

What makes it different

  • Unlocking takes a deliberate challenge you choose.
  • Usage data stays on your device.
  • Calls and emergencies are never blocked.
  • Six themes and six languages.
