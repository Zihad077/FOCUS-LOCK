Payment

How payment works

Payment is made with USDT through Binance Pay. Everything happens inside the app.

  1. Choose a plan in FocusLock.
  2. Complete the Binance payment for the exact amount shown.
  3. Submit the transaction ID in the app.
  4. The payment is verified.
  5. Premium is activated.

This website never asks for payment details, keys or wallet credentials.
