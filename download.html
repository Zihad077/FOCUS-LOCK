Help

Troubleshooting

The app will not install

Check that you have Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer and enough free space. Allow installs from unknown apps for your browser or file manager.

The block screen does not appear

Open Settings, then Permission Center, and make sure Usage access, Accessibility and Display over other apps are all granted.

The download does not start

Try another browser or network. You can also use the direct link above or get the file from GitHub.

Protection Limited banner on Home

This means a permission is missing, usually Accessibility. Tap Manage and turn it on.
