Choosing the right daily limit

Start from your real number

Look at how long you use the app today. Your first limit is a little less than that.

Lower it in small steps

Reduce the limit each week, not all at once.

Use different limits for different apps

An app you need for work gets a different limit than a game.

Review the trend

The Stats screen shows your daily trend. Adjust if you keep hitting the limit early.
