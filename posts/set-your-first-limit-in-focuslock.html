How to set your first limit in FocusLock

  1. Download FocusLock from the download page and install it.
  2. Grant the permissions the app asks for: Usage access, Accessibility and Display over other apps.
  3. Open the Apps tab and find an app you want to control.
  4. Switch it on and set a daily limit.
  5. Open Settings and choose the unlock challenges you want.

That is it. When the limit is reached, a block screen appears. See the app tour for every screen.
