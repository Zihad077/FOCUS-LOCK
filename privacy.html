Privacy policy

Items marked [CONFIG] depend on your setup and should be reviewed before launch.

Information collected

FocusLock stores your app limits, usage statistics, goals, streaks and settings in a local database on your device. There is no account.

Information not collected

FocusLock does not read your messages, passwords or keystrokes, and does not upload your usage history to its own servers. Analytics collection is turned off in the app.

Android permissions

Usage access, Accessibility, Display over other apps, Notifications, optional Do Not Disturb, and Internet. See the download page for what each is used for.

App usage and accessibility data

The Accessibility service is used to detect which app is in the foreground so the block screen can appear.

Local storage and backups

Data stays on your device. If you export a backup, the file is saved where you choose.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Adsterra in embedded web views. Adsterra may collect data under its own policy. Premium removes ads. [CONFIG: confirm ad partners]

Premium and payment information

Payments are made in Binance Pay. When you submit a transaction ID, the app checks it with Binance. FocusLock does not receive your Binance login. Premium expiry and used transaction IDs are stored on your device.

Third-party services

Adsterra (ads) and Binance (payment checks).

Data security

Data is kept on your device and protected by Android. No method is completely secure.

Children's privacy

FocusLock is not directed to children under 13. [CONFIG: review for your region]

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date below.
