Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FocusLock ("FocusLock", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the FocusLock Android application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

FocusLock is designed to keep your productivity data primarily on your device. Depending on the features you use, information stored locally may include:

  • App limits and selected applications.
  • App usage statistics used to calculate limits and progress.
  • Goals, streaks, and productivity settings.
  • FocusLock preferences and configuration.
  • Premium status and Premium expiration information.
  • Payment transaction references submitted for Premium verification.

We do not require you to create a FocusLock account to use the core application.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

FocusLock does not intentionally collect or upload the contents of your messages, passwords, keyboard input, personal conversations, photos, videos, or files through its core app-monitoring functionality.

Your app usage history and productivity information are not intentionally uploaded to FocusLock's own servers for general tracking or profiling.

3. App Usage Information

FocusLock needs to know which application is currently being used in order to enforce the limits you configure.

This information is used to calculate usage time, determine whether a selected application has reached its limit, and display the appropriate FocusLock protection screen.

App usage information used for these features is processed locally on your device unless a feature explicitly states otherwise.

4. Accessibility Service

FocusLock may use Android's Accessibility Service to detect the currently active application and display the FocusLock blocking interface when a configured limit is reached.

The Accessibility Service is not used to read the contents of messages, passwords, keyboard input, or other personal content.

Accessibility access is optional, but features that depend on it may not function correctly if the permission is not granted.

5. Android Permissions

Depending on the features you use, FocusLock may request the following Android permissions or special access:

  • Usage Access: Used to obtain application usage information needed for limits and statistics.
  • Accessibility Service: Used to detect the active application and enforce blocking.
  • Display over other apps: Used to display the FocusLock lock or blocking interface over a selected application.
  • Notifications: Used for FocusLock notifications and relevant reminders.
  • Do Not Disturb access: May be used by supported focus features when you explicitly enable them.
  • Internet access: Used for services such as advertisements, Premium payment verification, updates, and other online functionality.

You can manage these permissions through Android settings. Revoking a required permission may prevent the related FocusLock feature from working.

6. Local Storage and Backup

FocusLock stores its primary productivity settings and data locally on your device.

If FocusLock provides an export or backup feature, you choose where the exported file is saved. Exported files may contain FocusLock data, so you should keep them secure and avoid sharing them with people you do not trust.

Deleting the App or clearing its data may remove locally stored information unless you previously created an export or backup.

7. Advertising

The free version of FocusLock may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners.

Advertising providers may collect or process information such as device, network, advertising, or interaction information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

FocusLock does not control the information independently collected by third-party advertising providers.

Premium is designed to provide an ad-free experience for the active Premium period.

8. Premium and Payment Information

FocusLock offers optional Premium plans that may be purchased using Binance payment services.

When you submit a transaction ID or payment reference for Premium verification, that information may be sent to FocusLock's payment verification service to determine whether the payment is valid.

FocusLock does not request or store your Binance password, Binance account password, private keys, seed phrases, or wallet recovery credentials.

Payment verification may involve checking transaction information such as the transaction reference, payment amount, payment destination, transaction status, and the Premium plan associated with the payment.

9. Premium Entitlement

Information necessary to determine Premium status may be stored locally on your device and, where required for verification or restoration, on FocusLock's backend services.

Premium information may include the selected plan, activation status, expiration date, transaction reference, and information required to prevent the same transaction from being used multiple times.

We use this information to activate Premium, prevent fraudulent claims, and restore valid Premium access when supported.

10. Transaction IDs

Transaction IDs submitted for Premium verification are used only for payment verification, entitlement management, fraud prevention, support, and related operational purposes.

Do not submit passwords, private keys, recovery phrases, or other confidential credentials as part of a Premium payment verification request.

11. Third-Party Services

FocusLock may use third-party services for specific functions, including:

  • Binance or related payment infrastructure: Payment processing and transaction verification.
  • Advertising providers: Displaying advertisements in the free version.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: Operating FocusLock's website, backend services, or related infrastructure.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by FocusLock and its services.

However, no storage system, internet transmission, application, or electronic service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for protecting your device, exported backup files, and any information you use to access or manage FocusLock services.

13. Data Retention

Local FocusLock data generally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App's data, uninstall the App, or use an available deletion or reset feature.

Information processed by our backend services may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for payment verification, fraud prevention, service operation, security, dispute handling, or legal obligations.

14. Children's Privacy

FocusLock is a general productivity application and is not specifically directed toward children.

Users should only use FocusLock where permitted by applicable laws and the rules of their country or region.

15. Your Choices

You can control many aspects of FocusLock by changing your settings, disabling permissions, deleting locally stored data, uninstalling the App, or choosing whether to purchase Premium.

If you contact us about your personal information or a Premium transaction, we may need enough information to identify and investigate the relevant request.

16. Links and External Services

FocusLock and the FocusLock website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external services.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party service before providing it with personal information.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when FocusLock's features, services, advertising partners, payment systems, or legal requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page.
