7 simple ways to reduce screen time

Most of us do not plan to lose an hour to a feed. It just happens. These steps make that harder.

1. Pick the apps that cost you most

Check your usage stats and choose the two or three apps that eat the most time. Start with those, not everything.

2. Set a daily limit per app

A limit turns a vague wish into a number. In FocusLock you set a daily time limit for each app you choose.

3. Add friction

When the limit is reached, make opening the app slower. See why unlocking should take effort.

4. Protect blocks of deep work

Plan focus sessions with no notifications. Read how to run a deep focus session.

5. Track it weekly

Look at your trend once a week. Small drops add up.
